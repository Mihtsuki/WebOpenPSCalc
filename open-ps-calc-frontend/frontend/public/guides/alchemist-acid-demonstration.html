Alchemist — Acid Demonstration (SAD)

The SAD Alchemist — STR/DEX with plant summons and Acid Terror spam that works through DEF, brutal against high-DEF and high-HP targets. STR and DEX drive the throughput, AGI adds flee and plant attack speed, and it shines hardest under a Bard's Bragi.

Why this build works

Acid Terror works through DEF, so the monsters that shrug off weapon builds are exactly where it shines. Add plants that attack alongside you and the class turns into steady, DEF-proof throughput rather than big single hits.

Recommended stats (base level 99)

STR90
AGI60
VIT1
INT1
DEX80
LUK1

Summon, throw, repeat. Throughput is limited by cast and delay more than by damage, which is why an Alchemist gains more from a Bard's Bragi than almost any other class.

Skills that matter

  • Acid Terror — (100 + 100 × rank)% and works through DEF; 600% at rank 5.
  • Summon Flora / Marine Sphere — free damage that does not cost you casts.
  • Pharmacy — your supply line, and it scales some Alchemist gear.
  • Crazy Uproar — four ranks on Payon Stories: STR, VIT and soft DEF.

Gear

STR and DEX gear with enough AGI to keep plants and yourself moving. Bottles are the real cost of the build, so Pharmacy ranks are effectively damage.

Frequently asked questions

Does Acid Terror ignore DEF?

It works through defence rather than being reduced by it in the ordinary way, which is why it is the standard answer to high-DEF, high-HP targets.

How much damage does Acid Terror do at max rank?

Six hundred percent at rank five under the reworked formula of one hundred plus one hundred per rank.

Why do Alchemists want Bragi?

Because the build is limited by how often it can throw rather than by the damage of each throw. Reducing cast time and after-cast delay directly raises throughput.
