Hunter — Double Strafe (DS)

Single-target Double Strafe Hunter. Bow damage keys off DEX (maxed), with AGI maxed for attack speed and a little INT for the SP to keep spamming. Equip elemental arrows to match each target and you shred single targets from range.

Why this build works

Double Strafe is two hits from full range for one SP cost, and bow damage keys off DEX, which is also your hit and your cast speed for traps. Almost every point you spend does two jobs.

Range is the defence. Keep distance, let the falcon and traps do the work when things close, and swap arrows to match the target — arrow element is free damage that costs you nothing but inventory.

Skills that matter

  • Double Strafe — the single-target staple; two hits per cast.
  • Owl's Eye / Vulture's Eye — DEX and HIT, plus bow range.
  • Falcon — Blitz Beat procs on your bow attacks, scaling with LUK and INT and ignoring DEF.
  • Improve Concentration — AGI and DEX, so damage, hit and attack speed at once.

Gear

A composite bow you can slot, and a full set of elemental arrows. Arrows are the cheapest damage upgrade in the game — carrying five types beats a single better bow for most content.

Frequently asked questions

Do arrows change my damage element on Payon Stories?

Yes. The equipped arrow sets your attack element for physical hits, so matching arrows to the target is a straightforward multiplier that costs nothing but carrying them.

Is Double Strafe affected by the weapon size penalty?

Yes, like other physical attacks it is scaled by how well your bow suits the target's size before your status ATK is added.

Does a falcon help a Double Strafe Hunter?

Yes. Blitz Beat procs from your bow attacks for fixed damage that ignores DEF, and its chance scales with LUK, so even a modest investment adds steady damage.
