Monk — Asura

The burst Monk built around Asura Strike, whose damage is ATK × (8 + SP/10) + 1000 — so both ATK (STR) and Max SP (INT) matter. Spirit spheres add flat, true-neutral damage that stacks per hit. A high-ceiling nuke for MvPs and beefy targets.

Why this build works

Asura Strike is the biggest single hit available to a pre-renewal character, and its formula makes SP a damage stat: ATK × (8 + SP/10) + 1000. A Monk therefore builds STR and INT together, which is unusual enough that ordinary stat advice does not apply.

Everything is setup for one button. Gather spheres, get your SP full, land the combo, and detonate. Because the flat bonus is 1000 at every rank and the cast eats a fifth of your Max SP per rank, managing SP is the skill of the class.

Skills that matter

  • Asura Strike — ATK × (8 + ⌊SP/10⌋) + 1000. It does not ignore DEF on Payon Stories.
  • Call Spirits / Critical Explosion — spheres are +3 ATK each for the Monk line and feed the multiplier.
  • Triple Attack — five ranks here, procs on normal attacks, and can crit while Critical Explosion is up.
  • Finger Offensive — throws one sphere per rank; a useful single-target filler between Asuras.

Gear

Knuckles, STR for ATK, INT for the SP pool, and any gear that raises Max SP — it is literally damage. DEX enough to land the combo hits that set Asura up.

Frequently asked questions

How much SP do I need for a big Asura Strike?

As much as you can carry, because current SP is inside the multiplier: every ten SP adds another multiple of your ATK. The cast itself consumes twenty percent of your Max SP per rank.

Does Asura Strike ignore DEF?

Not on Payon Stories. The target's defence applies normally, unlike vanilla Ragnarok Online where the skill bypasses it.

Do spirit spheres increase Asura Strike?

Yes. Each active sphere adds three ATK for the Monk line, and because that lands on base ATK it is amplified by the skill's multiplier.
