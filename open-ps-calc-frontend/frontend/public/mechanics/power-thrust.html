Power-Thrust and the ATK buffs that stack with it

Power-Thrust adds its percentage into the skill ratio rather than multiplying the total — five ranks, +25% at most.

Five ranks, +5% each

Power-Thrust (also called Over Thrust) has five ranks and grants +5% ATK each, so +25% at maximum. It affects the caster and every party member on screen.

Crucially, the wiki notes that the bonus is additive for skills, not multiplicative: it is added into the skill's ratio percentage. So Cart Revolution Lv5, a 250% skill, becomes a 275% skill — not 250% × 1.25 = 312%. On a plain auto-attack (100%) it becomes 125%.

The rest of the Blacksmith buff kit

  • Maximum Power-Thrust — +20% ATK per level, but caster-only; it cannot be given to the party.
  • Adrenaline Rush — attack speed, not damage. On Payon Stories the caster gets +30% ASPD with an axe or mace and +20% with any other melee weapon; party members get +20% / +10%.
  • Weapon Perfection — removes the size penalty for the caster and the party.
  • Weaponry Research — passive: +2 HIT, +2 ATK and +2% accuracy per level.

Frequently asked questions

How much ATK does Power-Thrust give on Payon Stories?

Five percent per rank across five ranks, so twenty-five percent at maximum, for the caster and party members on screen.

Is Power-Thrust multiplied with the skill's damage?

No, it is added into the skill's ratio. A 250% skill under a maxed Power-Thrust becomes a 275% skill rather than being multiplied by 1.25.

Can Maximum Power-Thrust be cast on party members?

No. Maximum Power-Thrust affects only the caster; it is the Whitesmith's personal damage buff.
