How damage is calculated on Payon Stories

The order every physical hit passes through on Payon Stories — base damage, skill ratio, DEF, masteries, element, cards — and why the order changes the result.

The pipeline, in order

A physical hit is not one multiplication. It is a chain, and each step works on the result of the last one, so the order decides the number. Payon Stories keeps the pre-renewal order:

  1. Base damage — the weapon's ATK roll, the size penalty, then your status ATK (STR/DEX/LUK and gear) added on top.
  2. ATK rate — flat %ATK buffs.
  3. Skill ratio — the skill's own multiplier (Bash Lv10 = 400%, Cart Revolution Lv5 = 250%). Buffs like Power-Thrust are added into this percentage, not multiplied afterwards.
  4. Critical — a crit takes the weapon's maximum roll and skips DEF entirely.
  5. Defense — the target's hard DEF (a percentage cut) and then soft DEF (a flat subtraction).
  6. Refine and mastery — refine ATK and weapon-mastery ATK, both applied after DEF, which is why they are so strong against high-DEF targets.
  7. Element — your attack element against the target's element and level.
  8. Forge bonus — a forged weapon's Star Crumb damage, also after DEF.
  9. Cards — race, size, element and class card multipliers.
  10. Final rate — melee/ranged and per-weapon final modifiers.

Why the order matters

Two bonuses of the same size are not worth the same. Anything applied before DEF gets cut by it; anything applied after arrives whole. Against a high-DEF target, +40 ATK from a mastery is worth far more than +40 ATK on the weapon itself, because the mastery lands after the subtraction. It is also why refine and forge damage feel disproportionately good against armoured monsters — and why a crit, which skips DEF outright, is not simply "more damage" but a different calculation.

Frequently asked questions

Does a critical hit ignore DEF on Payon Stories?

Yes. A critical hit uses the weapon's maximum damage roll and bypasses the defence step entirely, which is why crit builds hold up so well against high-DEF monsters.

Are weapon mastery and refine damage reduced by the target's DEF?

No. Both are applied after the defence step, so they arrive at full value. That is why flat ATK from masteries and refine is worth more against armoured targets than the same amount of ATK on the weapon.

Do ATK buffs multiply the final damage?

Not usually. Power-Thrust and Over Thrust are added into the skill's ratio percentage rather than multiplied at the end, so a maxed Power-Thrust turns a 250% skill into a 275% skill.
