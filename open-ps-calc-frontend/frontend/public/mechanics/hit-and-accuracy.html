Hit, flee and accuracy on Payon Stories

How hit chance is computed, why skill accuracy bonuses are a percentage of your hit rate rather than flat HIT, and how much DEX you need to stop missing.

The hit formula

Your chance to land a hit is:

hit chance % = 80 + your HIT − the target's FLEE

capped at 100% and floored at 5% — you can always miss a little, and you can always connect a little. Your HIT is your base level plus your DEX, plus any flat +HIT on gear. A monster's FLEE is its level plus its AGI.

Accuracy is not HIT

Several skills grant "accuracy", and it is a different thing from +HIT. Accuracy is a percentage of your hit rate, applied after the subtraction above. The Payon Stories wiki puts it plainly: if you would hit a monster 67% of the time, Bash Lv10's +50% accuracy takes you to 100%, not to 117 HIT.

Multiple sources add together into a single multiplier rather than compounding, so Weaponry Research 10 and Holy Cross 10 give ×1.40, not ×1.44.

A worked example

Abysmal Knights are level 79 with 68 AGI, so 147 FLEE. At base level 99 your HIT is 99 + DEX. To never miss:

AttackDEX needed
Auto-attack68
Holy Cross Lv1 (+2%)67
Holy Cross Lv5 (+10%)59
Holy Cross Lv10 (+20%)52

That is 16 stat points of DEX bought purely by the skill's rank — which is why the accuracy line on a skill is worth reading before you plan your stats. The calculator's Hit breakpoints panel does this for your actual build and target.

Frequently asked questions

How much DEX do I need to never miss Abysmal Knights?

At base level 99 you need 52 total DEX when attacking with Holy Cross Lv10, or 68 DEX on a plain auto-attack. Abysmal Knights have 147 flee, and Holy Cross Lv10 adds 20% to your hit rate on top of your HIT.

Is skill accuracy the same as +HIT?

No. HIT is subtracted against the monster's flee to give a hit rate; accuracy multiplies that hit rate afterwards. A skill with +50% accuracy turns a 60% hit chance into 90%, whereas +50 HIT would raise the hit chance by 50 percentage points before the cap.

Can I reach 100% hit chance against everything?

Against most monsters yes, but perfect dodge is separate. A monster with high LUK can still dodge normal attacks outright, though that lucky dodge does not apply to skills.
